# Project Brief: Yum – Your World of Food

## 1. Executive Summary
**Yum** is an AI-native, global culinary ecosystem designed to transform how people discover, plan, shop, cook, and share food. Formerly known as *Culinary Intelligence*, the platform has evolved into a premium "FoodTech" social experience. It bridges the gap between digital inspiration and physical execution using advanced AI, gamification, and community-driven content.

---

## 2. Brand Identity
- **Name:** Yum
- **Tagline:** "Your World of Food" / "Smart Food. Smarter Cooking."
- **Brand Personality:** Warm, Friendly, Modern, Premium, Global, and Intelligent.
- **Visual Language (Yum Design System):**
    - **Primary Color:** Yum Orange (#e67e22) – evoking appetite and warmth.
    - **Palette:** Cream, warm neutrals, and deep accent tones.
    - **Typography:** IBM Plex Sans (Professional and readable).
    - **Shape Language:** Soft roundness (ROUND_EIGHT / ROUND_FULL) for an approachable feel.

---

## 3. Core Product Pillars

### A. AI & Intelligent Cooking
- **Yum AI:** A ubiquitous AI assistant available across all screens (Recipe, Pantry, Shopping, Challenges).
- **AI Recipe Generator:** Creates bespoke recipes based on available ingredients or specific cravings.
- **AI Cooking Mode:** Voice-controlled step-by-step guidance for hands-free kitchen operation.
- **Fusion Chef:** Merges different culinary traditions into entirely new flavor profiles.
- **AI Taste Profile (Culinary DNA):** Learns user preferences to provide hyper-personalized recommendations.

### B. Kitchen Management & Sustainability
- **Smart Pantry & Zero Waste:** Tracks inventory and suggests recipes to minimize food waste.
- **Smart Basket & Shopping Assistant:** Converts recipes into optimized shopping lists with budget-friendly suggestions.
- **Meal Planner:** Weekly automated meal scheduling based on health goals and taste.

### C. Global Discovery
- **Yum World Map:** An interactive globe for exploring regional cuisines and food cultures.
- **Yum Passport:** A gamified discovery system where users earn digital stamps for trying dishes from different countries.
- **Heritage Vault:** Preserves family recipes and traditional food stories.

### D. Community & Competition
- **Yum World Championship:** A global leaderboard-driven competition with seasonal challenges.
- **Yum Battles:** Live "Chef vs Chef" or "Human vs AI" cooking face-offs.
- **Yum Community:** A social feed for "Cooksnaps," recipe remixes, and following favorite creators.
- **Live Cooking Studio:** Tools for chefs to broadcast interactive cooking sessions with real-time ingredient overlays.

### E. Education
- **Yum Academy:** Professional-grade cooking courses, quizzes, and skill-level certifications.

---

## 4. Technical Features & UX
- **Modular Architecture:** Designed for global scalability and feature expansion.
- **Global Ready:** Multi-language support (RTL/LTR), currency selectors, and localized measurement units.
- **Smart Entry Experience:** A "Continue as Guest" flow that allows discovery before requiring account creation, coupled with a "Taste Onboarding" quiz.
- **Dark Mode Support:** Consistent premium experience across light and dark themes.
- **Micro-interactions:** Delightful animations for saving recipes (❤️), earning stamps (🌍), and victory in battles (🏆).

---

## 5. Vision for Expansion
- **Yum Market:** Integration with real-world grocery delivery services.
- **IoT Integration:** Connecting with smart kitchen appliances for automated temperature and time tracking.
- **AR Overlays:** Using augmented reality for precision plating and ingredient identification.
